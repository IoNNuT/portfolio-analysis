Romanian Fiscal Code clarifications
- Dividends/gains must be declared in "Declaratia Unica" even if the broker pays the taxes on behalf of the user. The user will not pay taxes to the government, he will just declare the earnings for other sources
- The tax on capital gains is 3% for positions > 365 days and 6% for < 365 days.
